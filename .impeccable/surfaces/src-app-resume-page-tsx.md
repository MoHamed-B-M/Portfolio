Scope: `/resume`, Read mode. Audience: recruiters and hiring managers. Job: verify education, projects, skills, and experience quickly, then print or save the CV. Proof: verified portfolio data only. Constraints: no emojis, no invented details, preserve rest of portfolio.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A one-page CV that reads like a recruitment document, not a portfolio hero. Refuses emoji, decorative icon tiles, cards-as-structure, and marketing metrics.

OWN-WORLD: A paper-like light ground with ink-dark text, hairline rules, generous margins, and one restrained blue accent for links and section markers. Typography is a workhorse system sans with a clear hierarchy; no display costume.

STORY: The reader sees identity and contact first, confirms current education, scans five concrete projects, verifies the 17-technology stack, then reads Enactus and Tunivision experience. A print action makes the same document portable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A centered A4-proportioned document on a quiet neutral backdrop. Header carries the exact name, role, and contact links. The first section below is Education, followed by Projects, Technical Skills, and Experience. The print action sits above the document, outside the paper.

FORM: Clean one-page document, the user-pinned layout. seed: none (pinned)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
