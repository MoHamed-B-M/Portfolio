Scope: `/cover-letter`, Read mode. Audience: recruiters and hiring managers. Job: understand Mohamed's motivation and fit, then print or save the letter as PDF. Proof: the user's exact draft, stored as editable data. Constraints: exact copy, no invented details, no emojis, dark screen / white-and-black print, preserve the rest of the portfolio.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A single A4 letter that reads like a physical document, not a portfolio section. Refuses dashboard chrome, icon tiles, decorative cards, and marketing language.

OWN-WORLD: An ink-dark screen with a dark paper surface, light text, hairline rules, generous margins, and a crisp white print action. Serif body text with disciplined tracking; no glow, no gradient decoration, no display costume.

STORY: The reader sees the name and contact block, the subject, then five paragraphs that move from education to technical range, teamwork, motivation, and close. The print action makes the same letter portable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A centered A4-proportioned letter on a quiet dark backdrop. Toolbar above with a back link and a high-contrast "Print / Download PDF" button. Header carries the exact name, role, and contact; subject and salutation follow immediately.

FORM: Clean one-page A4 document, the user-pinned form. seed: none (pinned) — "Design the cover letter to look like a professional, physical A4 document" and "Keep the dark mode aesthetic on the screen, but when printing, it must be a clean, standard document."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
